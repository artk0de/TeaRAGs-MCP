# Response-contract preregistration

Test the final-response contract privately before applying it. Freeze current plugin 0.53.8 as A; B differs only by appending `candidate.txt` once to `scripts/codex-rules.txt`. Use `gpt-6.1-sol`, medium, one fresh native actor per condition per case. The eight fixed cases are targeted1/2, batchB1/B2, architecture101/102 and guards201/202. Exact actor prompts and fixtures must match across arms after cwd/alias normalization. Keep grading outside actor input. Use alternating AB/BA order; no retries or follow-ups.

Apply only when aggregate native `reasoningOutputTokens` decreases by at least 20%, decreases on a strict majority of pairs with nonzero control reasoning, and all mandatory quality/instruction criteria pass in both arms. Zero-control pairs remain in aggregate and quality checks. Declare inconclusive if aggregate control reasoning is below 500 tokens, fewer than three controls are nonzero, telemetry is missing, control quality fails or paired inputs differ. These thresholds are fixed before candidate results.

Output tokens and wall time are secondary. Candidate must preserve evidence gathering and code locations. Explicit complete tables, inventories, code, plans and deep detail override the default 180-word/six-bullet cap; the two new guards directly test that exception. Root independently grades B1/B2 and those two new cases; the metric grader independently grades targeted1/2 and architecture101/102. No case replacement based on the floor or prior outcomes is permitted. Do not apply any production change before this gate passes. One sample per case/condition is exploratory evidence, not statistical proof. `prereg.json` contains the full frozen criteria and hashes.
