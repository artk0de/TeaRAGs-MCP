**They fail visibly, then can be resumed automatically after reconnect.** They do not remain indefinitely in `waiting` or `uploading`.

When the grant is rejected, affected uploads are marked:

```text
upload_status = failed
failure_reason = unauthorized
```

After a successful reconnect, the shared connection flow schedules `ResumeSendsAsync`. It selects **nondeleted documents belonging to that firm and provider** whose upload is `failed` with reason `unauthorized`, groups them by tax preparation, and passes the existing send records back to the adapter’s `ExportAsync`.

| Failure reason | Automatically resumed after reconnect? |
|---|---|
| `unauthorized` | **Yes** |
| `forbidden` — local permission denial | No |
| `network_error` — exhausted retries | No |
| `api_error` | No |
| `exceeded` | No |
| Other reasons | No |

Already-running sends are not selected. If the connection is no longer usable when the resume operation executes, it leaves the files failed for a later reconnect.

A successful background grant verification can also trigger this recovery, without requiring an admin reconnect.
