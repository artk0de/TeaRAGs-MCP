The sweep selects **successfully uploaded send records stuck in intermediate processing states**, separately for each registered provider.

| Filter | Default |
|---|---|
| Upload status | `uploaded` |
| Processing status `sent` | Uploaded **at least 1 hour ago** |
| Processing status `processing` | Uploaded **at least 12 hours ago** |
| Maximum age for either | Uploaded **no more than 2 days ago** |

All ages use **`uploaded_at`**, not creation time or the last status change. Boundaries are inclusive. Other processing states are excluded.

The thresholds can be overridden per provider, in seconds:

```yaml
tax_automations:
  <provider>:
    sent_after: 3600
    processing_after: 43200
    give_up_after: 172800
```

Candidates are grouped by firm. Reconciliation runs only if the firm has a **`connected` connection**, an Owner, and access to that provider through its feature flag. It runs as the Owner and delegates candidate IDs to `adapter::Documents::ReconcileStatusesAsync`.

Records older than the two-day window are **left unchanged**, not marked failed. The worker is intended to run every 30 minutes.
