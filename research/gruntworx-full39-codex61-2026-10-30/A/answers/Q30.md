**Contacts and accounts have UUID identifiers named `uid`; firms do not have an equivalent column in the checked migrations.**

| Entity | Table | UUID column |
|---|---|---|
| Firm | `firms` | No `uuid` or UUID-valued `uid` column found |
| Contact | `contacts` | `uid`, PostgreSQL type `uuid` |
| Account / client | `clients` | `uid`, PostgreSQL type `uuid` |

For contacts and clients, migrations establish:

- Default generation with `gen_random_uuid()`.
- A validated `uid IS NOT NULL` constraint.
- A unique index on `uid`.

For GWX URLs, **`contact.uid` is an existing UUID identifier**; use `client.uid` when identifying the account instead. A firm UUID would need to be introduced or stored in the GWX adapter’s mapping. `external_tenant_uuid` serves a different purpose: it identifies the **vendor’s account**, not the TaxDome firm.
