The shared flow **binds the firm to the vendor account reported by its first successful connection**.

The adapter returns a connection with `external_tenant_uuid`; the shared service compares it with `FirmSettings.external_tenant_uuid`:

| Existing binding | Vendor reports | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| None | Account A | Save A as the firm’s binding; accept connection. |
| Account A | Account A | Accept reconnect. |
| Account A | Account B | Reject with `account_mismatch`. |
| Any | No identifier | Skip the binding check; accept connection and preserve any existing binding. |

On mismatch, the connection transaction **rolls back**, preserving the previous settings and credentials. The service retains a copy of the newly obtained credentials and makes a **best-effort revocation** of that stray grant. Revocation failure is reported but does not change the rejection.

An explicit disconnect clears the binding, allowing a later connection to another account. Switching providers also clears the previous provider’s binding.

**If the vendor reports no account identifier, this protection cannot verify account identity.** The connection succeeds without establishing—or checking—a binding.
