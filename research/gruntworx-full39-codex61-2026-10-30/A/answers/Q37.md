Tax automations defines **nine application metrics** in the Yabeda `tax_automations` group:

| Metric | Type | Tags | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `requests_duration` | Histogram, seconds | `vendor`, `operation` | Duration of API calls, token exchanges and upload transfers |
| `failed_requests_total` | Counter | `vendor`, `operation`, `reason` | Failed request attempts |
| `rate_limit_remaining` | Gauge | `vendor`, `group` | Requests remaining from vendor rate-limit headers |
| `rate_limit_reset_seconds` | Gauge | `vendor`, `group` | Seconds until the vendor’s rate-limit window resets |
| `installation_changes_total` | Counter | `vendor`, `change` | Connection installation/uninstallation events |
| `reconciled_documents_total` | Counter | `vendor`, `processing_status` | Documents whose status reconciliation actually changed |
| `entity_matches_total` | Counter | `vendor`, `outcome` | Outcomes of matching a taxpayer to an existing vendor entity |
| `installations` | Gauge | `vendor` | Connections not explicitly disconnected, including expired/revoked ones |
| `stale_sends` | Gauge | `vendor` | Eligible uploaded sends still in `sent` beyond the reconciliation threshold |

Common tag values:

- **`operation`:** `api_request`, `connect`, `reconnect`, `token_refresh`, `token_revoke`, `upload_transfer`.
- **`reason`:** `HTTP_ERROR: <status>`, `NETWORK_ERROR: <exception class>`, `LOCK_TIMEOUT`, `INVALID_GRANT`; also `NO_PRINCIPAL` for revocation without a remaining principal.
- **`change`:** `installed`, `uninstalled`.
- **`outcome`:** `matched`, `matched_swapped`, `namesakes`, `swapped_namesakes`, `no_match`.

`installations` and `stale_sends` use a periodic collector with cached replica reads. `stale_sends` counts overdue **`sent`** records, not all reconciliation candidates.

A refused refresh can increment failures under both `token_refresh` and the dependent `api_request`; summing across operations does not give unique failed business actions.
