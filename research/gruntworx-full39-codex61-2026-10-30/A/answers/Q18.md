Both are defined in [`config/settings/features/tax_automations.yml`](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/settings/features/tax_automations.yml:1):

| Flag | Controls | Default | Scope / lifecycle |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tax_automations_unified_api` | Exposes `/api/v1/firm/tax_automations`. When off, the namespace returns `404`; firms continue using Juno endpoints. | `false` | Per firm, temporary; TD-95333 |
| `tax_automations_instead` | Offers Instead and permits starting its connection flow or sending documents. Existing connections can still be disconnected when off. | `false` | Per firm, permanent; TD-100346 |

The Instead flag is permanent because Instead is a US product and stays disabled on the EU cluster.

[`Registry::FEATURE_FLAGS`](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/lib/tax_preparation/tax_automations/registry.rb:13) maps `instead` to `:tax_automations_instead`; `provider_available?` and `sending_available?` apply that gate.

Neither flag selects mock transport.
