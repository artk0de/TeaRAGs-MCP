**Both, with different roles:** `client_id` identifies the TaxDome account; `contact_id` identifies the person within that account.

- **Personal return:** both are required. The contact must belong to the specified account.
- **Business return:** `client_id` is required; `contact_id` may be omitted. The company is described by its business name and EIN.
- Documents are selected and authorized under the **account**, not the contact.

The shared tax-preparation record is looked up by **`(firm_id, client_id, contact_id, year)`**. `return_type` is mutable metadata, not part of that lookup key.
