TaxDome compares in **binary MiB**. Validation uses:

```ruby
limit = terms.max_file_size_mb.megabytes
document.file_size > limit
```

Rails `.megabytes` multiplies by **1,048,576**. Therefore `max_file_size_mb: 50` means **52,428,800 bytes (50 MiB)**, matching the vendor’s limit. A file exactly that size is accepted.

The field name says `mb`, but its effective unit is MiB. See [validation](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/services/tax_preparation/tax_automations/export/validate_documents.rb:142).
