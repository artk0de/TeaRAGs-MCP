**Both, with the TaxDome account (`client_id`) always required.**

The shared `ExportAsync` takes:

- `client_id` — required; identifies the account and scopes document selection.
- `contact_id` — required for a **personal return**; identifies the taxpayer within that account.
- For a **business return**, the company is the taxpayer, so no contact is required; business details include the EIN.

The shared envelope lookup uses **firm + client + contact + tax year**. The adapter translates that identity into its vendor-specific taxpayer model.
