Two flags govern the shared tax-automation API and Instead. Both are defined in [`config/settings/features/tax_automations.yml`](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/settings/features/tax_automations.yml:1).

| Flag | Controls | Default | Lifecycle |
|---|---|---|---|
| `tax_automations_unified_api` | Access to `/api/v1/firm/tax_automations`; when off, that namespace returns 404 | Disabled | Temporary, firm-scoped; TD-95333 |
| `tax_automations_instead` | Offering Instead: starting authorization and sending documents. Existing connections can still disconnect when off | Disabled | Permanent, firm-scoped; TD-100346 |

The provider registry maps `instead` to `tax_automations_instead` in [`FEATURE_FLAGS`](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/lib/tax_preparation/tax_automations/registry.rb:13). Provider availability also gates reconciliation.

These are feature flags; the proposed mock-transport setting is a separate provider configuration.
