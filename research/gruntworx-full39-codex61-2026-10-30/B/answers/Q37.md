The `tax_automations` Yabeda group defines **nine metrics**:

| Metric | Type | Labels | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `requests_duration` | Histogram, seconds | `vendor`, `operation` | API, token-exchange and upload-transfer duration |
| `failed_requests_total` | Counter | `vendor`, `operation`, `reason` | Failed requests |
| `rate_limit_remaining` | Gauge | `vendor`, `group` | Remaining budget from vendor response headers |
| `rate_limit_reset_seconds` | Gauge | `vendor`, `group` | Seconds until the rate-limit window resets |
| `installation_changes_total` | Counter | `vendor`, `change` | Connection installations and uninstallations |
| `reconciled_documents_total` | Counter | `vendor`, `processing_status` | Sends whose status reconciliation actually changed |
| `entity_matches_total` | Counter | `vendor`, `outcome` | Results of taxpayer-name matching |
| `installations` | Gauge | `vendor` | Connections not explicitly disconnected; includes expired/revoked |
| `stale_sends` | Gauge | `vendor` | Uploaded sends still `sent` past the reconciliation threshold, with a reconcilable connection |

Label values include:

- **`operation`:** `api_request`, `connect`, `reconnect`, `token_refresh`, `token_revoke`, `upload_transfer`.
- **`reason`:** `HTTP_ERROR: <status>`, `NETWORK_ERROR: <class>`, `LOCK_TIMEOUT`, `INVALID_GRANT`, `NO_PRINCIPAL`.
- **`change`:** `installed`, `uninstalled`.
- **`outcome`:** `matched`, `matched_swapped`, `namesakes`, `swapped_namesakes`, `no_match`.

`installations` and `stale_sends` are collected only in Sidekiq processes, using replica reads cached for 60 seconds. **`stale_sends` counts stale `sent` records only**, although reconciliation also handles stale `processing` records.
