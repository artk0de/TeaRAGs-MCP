The sweep runs **every 30 minutes** and considers each registered provider separately.

A send becomes a candidate when:

| Upload status | Processing status | Threshold based on `uploaded_at` |
|---|---|---|
| `uploaded` | `sent` | At least **1 hour** old |
| `uploaded` | `processing` | At least **12 hours** old |

Both groups are restricted to uploads **no older than 2 days**. Older sends remain unchanged; other processing states are excluded.

Each provider can override these defaults, in seconds:

```yaml
tax_automations:
  <provider>:
    sent_after: 3600
    processing_after: 43200
    give_up_after: 172800
```

Candidates are grouped by firm. Reconciliation proceeds only if that provider has a **`connected` connection**, the firm has an owner, and the provider is available under its feature flag. The sweep then calls the adapter’s `Documents::ReconcileStatusesAsync`.

The age is measured from **upload completion**, not the last status change.
