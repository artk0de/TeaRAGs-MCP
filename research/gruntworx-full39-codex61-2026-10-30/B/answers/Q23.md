For Instead, routing has two layers:

1. **Cluster middleware:** [`Middleware::Clusters::Webhooks::WEBHOOKS_MAP`](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/lib/middleware/clusters/webhooks.rb:49) explicitly maps:

   ```ruby
   '/webhooks/instead' => :no_proxy
   ```

   The receiving cluster handles the request locally. It is **not broadcast or forwarded to another cluster**, and the payload is not inspected to choose one. The vendor must therefore call the correct cluster’s endpoint.

2. **Rails controller routing:** On the app domain, [`config/routes/tax_preparation.rb`](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/routes/tax_preparation.rb:733) maps:

   ```text
   POST /webhooks/instead/events
     → TaxPreparation::Webhooks::TaxAutomations::Instead::EventsController#create
   ```

   `scope module: :tax_automations` adds the controller namespace without adding `/tax_automations` to the URL.

After signature verification and validation, the controller delegates to `ProcessEventAsync`, which resolves the **local firm** from the entity/file mapping for document events, or the external tenant binding for entity events.

For a second vendor, both its Rails route and its explicit cluster-proxy policy need to be defined.
