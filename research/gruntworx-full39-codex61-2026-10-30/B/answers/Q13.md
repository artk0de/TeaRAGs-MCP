The shared connection flow **binds the firm to the external account reported on its first successful connection**.

Inside the connection transaction, it compares `connection.external_tenant_uuid` with `FirmSettings.external_tenant_uuid`:

| Vendor reports | Behavior |
|---|---|
| An identifier, with no existing binding | Store it as the firm’s binding |
| The same identifier | Allow reconnection |
| A different identifier | Reject with `account_mismatch` and roll back the connection transaction |
| No identifier | Skip the check; allow connection and leave any existing binding unchanged |

After a mismatch, TaxDome attempts to **revoke the newly obtained grant** on a best-effort basis. Revocation failures are reported; the mismatch remains rejected.

Switching to a **different provider** clears the old account binding before binding the new provider’s account.

If a vendor never reports an account identifier, this protection cannot detect reconnection to a different account.
