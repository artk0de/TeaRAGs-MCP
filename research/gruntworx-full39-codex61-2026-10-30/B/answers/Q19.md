The shared [`TaxAutomations::Document`](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/models/tax_preparation/tax_automations/document.rb:39) has two independent AASM machines.

**Processing status** — initial state: `sent`

| Event | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| `start_processing` | `sent`, `error` | `processing` |
| `complete_processing` | `sent`, `processing` | `processed` |
| `fail_processing(reason = nil)` | `sent`, `processing` | `error` |
| `exceed_limit` | `sent`, `processing` | `exceeded` |
| `archive` | `sent`, `processing`, `processed`, `error`, `exceeded` | `archived` |
| `resend` | `processing`, `processed`, `error`, `archived`, `exceeded` | `sent` |

`fail_processing` stores the supplied failure reason; `exceed_limit` sets `exceeded`. Transitions into states other than `error` or `exceeded` clear the reason.

There is no direct `error → processed` transition: the adapter must go through `processing`.

**Upload status** — initial state: `waiting`

| Event | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| `upload` | `waiting`, `uploaded`, `failed` | `uploading` |
| `finish` | `uploading` | `uploaded` |
| `fail(reason)` | `waiting`, `uploading` | `failed` |
| `reset` | `uploading`, `uploaded`, `failed` | `waiting` |

`finish` stamps `uploaded_at`; `fail` stores its reason. Every upload transition except one into `failed` clears the reason.

Both machines share the **same `failure_reason` column**. Invalid transitions raise. The processing helper `transition_to!` treats a report of the current state as a no-op.
