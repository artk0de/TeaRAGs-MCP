**They fail first, then eligible files are retried after reconnection.** They do not remain waiting.

When the grant is rejected mid-send, affected uploads are marked `failed` with failure reason **`unauthorized`**. The worker stops retrying that operation and can trigger grant verification.

After a successful connection, the shared flow schedules `ResumeSendsAsync`. It selects:

- The same firm and provider.
- Upload status `failed`.
- Failure reason **`unauthorized` only**.
- Documents that have not been deleted.

It groups those sends by tax preparation and calls the adapter’s `ExportAsync` again, provided the connection is still usable.

Failures such as `network_error`, `api_error`, `exceeded`, `forbidden`, or `unexpected` are **not automatically resumed by reconnection**. Neither are processing failures on files whose upload already succeeded.
