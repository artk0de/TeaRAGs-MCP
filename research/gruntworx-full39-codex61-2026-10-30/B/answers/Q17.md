Yes: [`firm_site_backups`](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/storage.yml:30) supports its own bucket and credentials:

- `FIRM_SITE_BACKUPS_S3_ACCESS_KEY_ID`
- `FIRM_SITE_BACKUPS_S3_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`
- `FIRM_SITE_BACKUPS_S3_BUCKET`

It uses `S3Cutover` and the shared `AWS_REGION`.

**Caveat:** those settings fall back to the main S3 credentials and bucket. A dedicated vendor-facing service could copy this structure, but omit those fallbacks if isolation is required.

`amazon_tax_software_backups` also has a separate bucket setting, but inherits the main credentials.
