The provider’s webhook URL is configured to call **`POST /webhooks/instead/events`**. The route is under `RouteConstraints::AppDomainConstraint` and defaults to `HostHelper.app_subdomain`, so the vendor must send deliveries to the **app host for the relevant TaxDome cluster**. The constraint also accepts configured app-private hosts and Kubernetes hosts.

The route maps to `TaxPreparation::Webhooks::TaxAutomations::Instead::EventsController#create` via the route’s `tax_preparation` module scope. It is a provider-specific endpoint, rather than a shared vendor webhook route.
