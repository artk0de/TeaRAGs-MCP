Files do **not** stay waiting when the vendor rejects the grant mid-send. The affected send records are marked `failed` with reason `unauthorized`. After a successful reconnect, TaxDome finds failed sends for that provider with that reason and submits them again. If the grant is unusable when the resume job runs, they remain failed for a later reconnect.

Only failures with reason **`unauthorized`** are resumed. That includes a rejected or invalid grant, an HTTP `401`, or insufficient OAuth scope. Other failures—such as `forbidden`, network or API errors, and quota exceeded—are not automatically resumed by reconnect.
