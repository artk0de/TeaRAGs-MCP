The hierarchy is:

```text
TaxPreparation::TaxAutomations::OperationWorker
└── Instead::OperationWorker
    ├── Instead::ExportOperationWorker
    │   ├── send-step workers (create entity, start upload, finalize upload)
    │   └── TransferBytesWorker
    └── Webhooks::ProcessEventWorker
```

- **Send-step workers** use `Instead::ExportOperationWorker`, which is `step_only!` and inherits the shared operation retry policy.
- **`TransferBytesWorker` also uses `ExportOperationWorker`**, even though it transfers bytes to a signed URL and doesn’t use the Instead API throttle.
- **`Webhooks::ProcessEventWorker` extends `Instead::OperationWorker` directly**. It doesn’t use the export-step failure handler because it isn’t a send step.

When a send step exhausts retries, `ExportOperationWorker` marks its named files `failed`: terminal refusals get their mapped failure reason; other exhausted errors become `network_error`. Canceled or missing operations are skipped. The webhook worker has no send files to fail; its retries follow the shared operation policy.
