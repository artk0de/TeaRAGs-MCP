- **Shared `ApplicationWorker`** sets the default queue to `tax_preparation_tax_automations_default`. It does not set retries, throttle, or a slug.
- **Shared `OperationWorker`** sets the same default queue and `retry: 10`. It kills retries for missing records, policy errors, and invalid grants; `RateLimitedError` retries after the vendor’s `Retry-After` value. On invalid-grant retry exhaustion, it starts grant verification. It does **not** declare a slug or throttle group; adapter operation classes provide those.
- **Instead `OperationWorker`** defines the adapter-specific throttle arguments: rate-limit group plus connection ID, with `default` as the fallback group. It also provides the hook for subclasses to declare their rate-limit group. It does not define a slug; each concrete Instead operation worker declares its own.

The throttle registry is in [config/initializers/sidekiq.rb](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/initializers/sidekiq.rb:106). The per-connection group limits and app-wide requests-per-minute limit are in [config/settings.yml](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/settings.yml:1817), with production overrides in [config/settings/production.yml](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/settings/production.yml:187). The registry applies both a **per-connection, per-group** limit and an **app-wide** limit. Production sets the app-wide limit to 3,000 requests/minute and `docs` to 60/minute; development defaults are 500 app-wide and 5 `docs` requests/minute. The throttle only applies to workers that opt in with `sidekiq_throttle_as :tax_automations_instead`.
