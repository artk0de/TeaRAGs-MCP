Use **`Crm::Api::Contact.find(firm:, user:, actor:, contact_id:)`** with the numeric Contact ID from the tax-preparation record. Its returned contact data includes `first_name`, `last_name`, and `uid`—the stable UUID.

`Crm::Api::Contact.list_by_account(...)` is used to check which contacts belong to an account, but its result only includes `contact_id`, `phone`, and `name`; it does **not** return the UUID or separate first and last names. Tax automations already use `Contact.find` for the selected taxpayer in Instead’s entity creation and matching flows.
