The send record has two independent AASM state machines, both starting in their first listed state.

### Processing status

Starts at `sent`.

| Event | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| `start_processing` | `sent`, `error` | `processing` |
| `complete_processing` | `sent`, `processing` | `processed` |
| `fail_processing` | `sent`, `processing` | `error` |
| `exceed_limit` | `sent`, `processing` | `exceeded` |
| `archive` | `sent`, `processing`, `processed`, `error`, `exceeded` | `archived` |
| `resend` | `processing`, `processed`, `error`, `archived`, `exceeded` | `sent` |

### Upload status

Starts at `waiting`.

| Event | From | To |
|---|---|---|
| `upload` | `waiting`, `uploaded`, `failed` | `uploading` |
| `finish` | `uploading` | `uploaded` |
| `fail` | `waiting`, `uploading` | `failed` |
| `reset` | `uploading`, `uploaded`, `failed` | `waiting` |

Failure reasons are cleared after transitions unless the resulting processing state is `error`/`exceeded` or the resulting upload state is `failed`. `finish` also sets `uploaded_at`. Source: [document.rb](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/models/tax_preparation/tax_automations/document.rb:85).
