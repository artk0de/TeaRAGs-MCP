The shared send entry point identifies the taxpayer by the **TaxDome account (`client_id`)**. It uses that account for authorization, document ownership, and creating or finding the tax preparation record.

For a personal return, it also requires a **`contact_id`** to identify the person associated with the account. For a business return, the contact may be absent: the send is tied to the account and business details such as EIN. So `client_id` anchors the send; `contact_id` adds the individual taxpayer when needed.
