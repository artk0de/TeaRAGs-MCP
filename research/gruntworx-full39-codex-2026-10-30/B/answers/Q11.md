The sweep considers **uploaded** send records only; it does not pick up records still waiting or uploading. It selects:

- `sent` records at least **1 hour** after `uploaded_at`.
- `processing` records at least **12 hours** after `uploaded_at`.

Both thresholds have a **2-day give-up window**: records older than that are no longer candidates. These are defaults; each provider can override `sent_after`, `processing_after`, and `give_up_after` in `Settings.tax_automations.<provider>` (seconds).

Candidates are grouped by firm and handed to that provider’s reconciliation service if the firm has a connected grant, an owner, and the provider is available. The sweep runs every 30 minutes.
