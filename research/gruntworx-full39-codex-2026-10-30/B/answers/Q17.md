Yes. `config/storage.yml` has two relevant patterns:

- **`amazon_tax_software_backups`** uses its own bucket (`S3_TAX_SOFTWARE_BACKUPS_BUCKET`) while inheriting shared S3 credentials from `amazon_defaults`.
- **`firm_site_backups`** has purpose-specific credential settings (`FIRM_SITE_BACKUPS_S3_ACCESS_KEY_ID` and `...SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`) and a bucket setting. Those credentials and bucket fall back to the shared S3 values if unset.

For a dedicated vendor-facing bucket with separate credentials, `firm_site_backups` is the closer template for credentials; combine it with the tax-software-backups pattern of a dedicated bucket and development MinIO service. The tax-software-backups service alone does **not** use separate credentials. [config/storage.yml](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/storage.yml:26)
