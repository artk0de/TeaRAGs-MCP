- **Send status serializers:**
  - `TaxPreparation::Base::TaxAutomations::DocumentSerializer` exposes a send record directly, including `status` (the processing status), `upload_status`, `failure_reason`, and `failure_message`. The API serializer for the tax-automation documents index uses it.
  - `TaxPreparation::Base::DocumentSerializer` and `TaxPreparation::Base::DocumentChecklist::ItemSerializer` expose the latest send’s status through optional included fields: `tax_automation_status`, `tax_automation_upload_status`, `tax_automation_failure_reason`, and `tax_automation_failure_message`. These fields require `read_status` authorization.
