For the two **Instead adapter tables**:

| Table | Indexes | Foreign keys |
|---|---|---|
| `tax_preparation_tax_automations_instead_connections` | Unique `firm_id`; partial `refresh_expires_at` index where `status = 1` (connected). | None. `firm_id` is a soft reference because firms are in another cluster; `creator` is polymorphic. |
| `tax_preparation_tax_automations_instead_documents` | Index on `instead_entity_id`; unique `tax_automation_document_id`; unique `firm_id, external_uuid` where the UUID is present; and unique `external_uuid` where present. | `tax_automation_document_id` → shared tax-automation documents, **ON DELETE CASCADE**; `instead_entity_id` → Instead entities. No FK on `firm_id`. |

The standalone unique `external_uuid` index supports webhook lookup by vendor file UUID without knowing the firm first. The earlier composite unique index also enforces uniqueness within a firm.
