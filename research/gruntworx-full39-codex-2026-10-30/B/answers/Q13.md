The first successful connection binds the firm to the vendor account identifier (`external_tenant_uuid`) returned with the grant. On reconnecting to that same provider, TaxDome compares the new identifier with the stored one. If it differs, the reconnect is refused as `account_mismatch`, and TaxDome makes a best-effort attempt to revoke the stray grant. A provider switch clears the old vendor’s binding before the new account is bound.

If the vendor reports **no account identifier**, the flow skips binding and mismatch checks: there’s no identifier to compare. That means TaxDome can’t use this safeguard for that provider. The logic is in [connections/create.rb](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/services/tax_preparation/tax_automations/connections/create.rb:90).
