The existing vendor specs use the **`json-schema` gem**, through `JSON::Validator`, to validate payloads against named component schemas in the Instead OpenAPI contract. A shared helper loads the YAML contract and adds a `$ref` to the selected component so its internal references resolve.

The client specs validate both fixture payloads—`Entity`, `File`, `SignedUpload`, and `TokenResponse`—and some parsed responses from mocked client calls, including entities, files, and signed upload targets.

**The in-process `Instead::Mock::Router` answers are not currently validated this way.** I found no specs that call the router or use `conforms_to_contract?` on its output. So the contract checks help prevent fixture/response drift, but they do not currently catch drift in that mock transport.
