- **Contacts:** yes. `contacts.uid` is a UUID. It was added as nullable, with a UUID default added in a later migration.
- **Accounts:** yes. The account model is stored in the `clients` table, which has a `uid` UUID column with the same migrations.
- **Firms:** I found no `uuid` or `uid` column on `firms`.

So TaxDome has stable UUID identifiers for both contacts and accounts, but not firms. `FirmSettings.external_tenant_uuid` is the **vendor’s** account identifier, not a TaxDome firm ID. If GruntWorx needs a stable TaxDome firm identifier in its URLs, the current schema doesn’t provide one on `firms`.
