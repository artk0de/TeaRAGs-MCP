There are two firm-scoped feature flags, both defined in [config/settings/features/tax_automations.yml](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/settings/features/tax_automations.yml:1):

- **`tax_automations_unified_api`** — gates the unified tax-automations API. When off, its endpoints return 404 and firms continue using the Juno endpoints.
- **`tax_automations_instead`** — gates offering Instead: when off, firms can’t start an Instead connection or send documents through it, but an existing connection can still be disconnected.

The provider flag is also mapped in the registry’s `FEATURE_FLAGS` constant: `instead` → `tax_automations_instead`.
