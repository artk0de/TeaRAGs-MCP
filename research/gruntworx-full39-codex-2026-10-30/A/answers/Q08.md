TaxDome treats `max_file_size_mb` as **binary MiB**: the export validator multiplies it by ActiveSupport’s `.megabytes`, which is `1,048,576` bytes. The check rejects a file only when its byte size is **greater than** that limit.

So a GWX limit of **50 MiB** maps directly to `max_file_size_mb: 50` (52,428,800 bytes). The passport field is an integer count of megabytes, documented in the contract as the unit used for file-size checks: [HasCapabilities](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/models/tax_preparation/tax_automations/has_capabilities.rb:36); the comparison is in [ValidateDocuments](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/services/tax_preparation/tax_automations/export/validate_documents.rb:112).
