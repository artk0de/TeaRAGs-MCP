Two flags govern the flow:

- `tax_automations_unified_api` gates the shared tax-automations API. It is defined in [config/settings/features/tax_automations.yml](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/settings/features/tax_automations.yml:2).
- `tax_automations_instead` gates offering the Instead provider to a firm. It is defined in the same file, and the registry maps provider `instead` to that flag in [registry.rb](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/lib/tax_preparation/tax_automations/registry.rb).

The settings currently define both flags as disabled.
