The shared send entry point identifies the **TaxDome client account** with `client_id`. It also takes a `contact_id` for a personal return, and carries both into the tax-preparation record. For a business return, the taxpayer is the company, so no person contact is required.

So it’s **client account as the root TD identity, with a contact for personal returns**—not contact alone. The entry-point contract documents `client_id` as required and `contact_id` as required for personal returns; the implementation uses the client to create or find the preparation, then passes both IDs into it ([ExportAsync](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/services/tax_preparation/tax_automations/export_async.rb:22), [parameter mapping](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/services/tax_preparation/tax_automations/export_async.rb:196)).
