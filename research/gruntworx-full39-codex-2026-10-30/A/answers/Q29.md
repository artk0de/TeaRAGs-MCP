The shared export service first authorizes the member for `:export`. Then `Instead::ExportAsync` fetches the firm’s **usable connection** and separately authorizes `:read` on that connection. Its service spec allows the owner and refuses employees; the two checks protect different things: permission to send, and access to the grant.

It passes only `connection_id`, `tax_preparation_id`, and the send-record IDs to `ExportAsyncWorkflow.perform_async_operation`. The workflow is enqueued after the shared send transaction commits, so a rolled-back send starts no work ([adapter service](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/services/tax_preparation/tax_automations/instead/export_async.rb:28)).

The workflow reuses an existing Instead entity if found, links one by name if possible, or starts an entity-creation step. Once it has an entity, it prepares the records and starts one upload worker per document ([workflow](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/workers/tax_preparation/tax_automations/instead/documents/export_async_workflow.rb:22), [upload steps](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/workers/tax_preparation/tax_automations/instead/documents/export_async_workflow.rb:41)).
