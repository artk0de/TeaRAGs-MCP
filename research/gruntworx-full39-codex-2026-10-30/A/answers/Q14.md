They **fail first** with `upload_status: failed` and `failure_reason: unauthorized`; they don’t stay waiting. On a successful reconnect, the connection flow calls the resume service, which re-enqueues eligible files through the provider adapter’s export flow ([connection flow](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/services/tax_preparation/tax_automations/connections/create.rb:49), [resume service](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/services/tax_preparation/tax_automations/documents/resume_sends.rb:33)).

Only failed, non-deleted files with **`unauthorized`** are resumed. A user or permission refusal (`forbidden`), exhausted network retries (`network_error`), other failure reasons, and sends still in progress are not. If the new grant is already unusable when resumption runs, files stay failed for a later reconnect.
