### Shared operation base

`TaxPreparation::TaxAutomations::OperationWorker` sets `RETRY_ATTEMPTS = 10` and defaults to the `tax_preparation_tax_automations_default` queue with Sidekiq retry set to 10. Its retry ladder runs for about 4.5 hours; a throttled job rescheduled by the throttle starts a new retry count. The shared base does **not** declare a slug or an Instead throttle ([shared base](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/workers/tax_preparation/tax_automations/operation_worker.rb:3)).

### Instead operation base and throttle

`Instead::OperationWorker` inherits those retry settings. It adds throttle arguments containing the connection ID and rate-limit group, and maps the group through `Instead::RateLimitGroup`. Its default group is `default`. It does **not** declare a slug; each concrete operation worker declares its own ([Instead base](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/workers/tax_preparation/tax_automations/instead/operation_worker.rb:3)).

The throttle is registered in [config/initializers/sidekiq.rb](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/initializers/sidekiq.rb:107) under `:tax_automations_instead`. It applies two one-minute limits, in this order:

1. Per rate-limit group and connection, using the group’s configured limit.
2. App-wide, using `requests_per_minute`.

The limits live under `tax_automations.instead` in [config/settings.yml](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/settings.yml:1819). Defaults are 500 app requests/minute and per-group limits of 5 for `docs`, 50 for `entities`, 10 for `agents`, 50 for `webhooks`, 50 for `scim`, and 50 for `default`. Production overrides the app limit to 3,000 and `docs` to 60 in [production.yml](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/settings/production.yml:187).
