Incoming webhooks first pass through `Middleware::Clusters::Webhooks` on the **main cluster**. It classifies `POST` paths by prefix: `:sync` proxies to clusters and returns a successful response; `:async` acknowledges immediately and proxies in the background; `:no_proxy` stays on the main cluster.

Instead’s prefix, `/webhooks/instead`, is configured as `:no_proxy`, so its webhook is **not fanned out or sent to another cluster**. Rails then routes `POST /webhooks/instead/events` to `TaxPreparation::Webhooks::TaxAutomations::Instead::EventsController#create` ([cluster routing](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/lib/middleware/clusters/webhooks.rb:7), [route](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/routes/tax_preparation.rb:753), [controller](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/app/controllers/tax_preparation/webhooks/tax_automations/instead/events_controller.rb:6)).
