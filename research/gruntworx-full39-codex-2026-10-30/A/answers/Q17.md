Yes. [config/storage.yml](/Users/Shared/nav-ab/repo-master/config/storage.yml:29) has the purpose-scoped `amazon_tax_software_backups` service. It inherits the shared S3Cutover configuration and its own bucket from `S3_TAX_SOFTWARE_BACKUPS_BUCKET`; credentials come from the shared `S3_ACCESS_KEY_ID` and `S3_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`.

There’s also `firm_site_backups`, which has its own credential variables with fallback to shared S3 credentials. Either is a pattern for a dedicated bucket; the tax-software-backups service is the closest example of a separate bucket with shared credentials.
